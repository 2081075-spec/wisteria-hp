COLD-PRESSED & HPP

KHARIS

Smoothies made by cold pressing and High Pressure Processing. No heating step, no additives.

PROCESS

How it is made

  1. 01Cold PressJuice is extracted at low temperature to limit frictional heat.
  2. 02BottlingFreshly pressed juice is filled into pouches and sealed.
  3. 03HPP Sterilization6,000 bar for three minutes inactivates microorganisms without heat.
  4. 04Cold ChainKept chilled or frozen from dispatch to delivery.

6,000 bar is the pressure found at a depth of 60,000 metres. Because there is no heat sterilization (80 °C or above), the colour changes and off-notes caused by heating do not occur.
