BUSINESS

Business

Our business lines are diverse, but the goal is the same: to connect Japan's resources and products with the people around the world who need them. We currently run seven lines in five fields.

01  RESOURCESUrban mining

Metal Recovery

Urban Mining

We recover precious and non-ferrous metals, mainly from used printed circuit boards. Metal content varies with the board type and the components mounted on it, so we inspect the actual material before we quote, and we disclose the basis of every assessment.

About metal recovery

02  INGREDIENTSFood ingredients

Food Ingredients & Additives

Import & Wholesale

Under authorised distributor agreements with two manufacturers in China, we import 28 products, starting with GDL (glucono-delta-lactone), directly from the manufacturers. We supply coagulants, sweeteners, dietary fibre, amino acids and feed ingredients to trading companies and manufacturers in Japan.

About food ingredients

03  GASTRONOMYFood & beverage

KHARIS

Cold-pressed & HPP

Smoothies made with cold pressing and HPP (High Pressure Processing), with no heating step and no additives. Four varieties in 200 g pouches.

About KHARIS

Wisteria WAGYU

Wagyu Beef

Wholesale and export of branded wagyu beef in partnership with certified processing plants in Japan. We propose cuts by application and cut to order. A halal-compliant supply chain is in preparation.

About Wisteria WAGYU

04  CULTURECollectible vehicles

Wisteria GARAGE

Vehicle Export

Export of used and classic Japanese cars. Every vehicle is inspected before it ships. Japanese classics are sought after overseas for their condition and rarity.

05  TECHNOLOGYDrones & equipment

Industrial Drone

Inspection & Maintenance

High-pressure cleaning and exterior wall inspection, solar panel inspection, snow melting, and spraying heat-shield coatings on barns and similar structures. Work at height is done without scaffolding.

For building exteriors we offer SORAMIRU, a scheduled drone patrol service in which AI detects cracks and corrosion in the drone imagery.
