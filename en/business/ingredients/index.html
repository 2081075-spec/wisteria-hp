Food Ingredients & Additives

Direct from the manufacturer to Japan.

Under authorised distributor agreements for Japan with two manufacturers in China, we import 28 products, starting with GDL (glucono-delta-lactone), directly from the manufacturers. We supply them wholesale to trading companies and manufacturers in Japan.

How supply works

We deal directly with our partner manufacturers, without going through agents or export traders in China. Wisteria acts as the importer and delivers to customers in Japan.
