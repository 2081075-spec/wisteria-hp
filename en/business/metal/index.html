Metal Recovery

Making hidden value visible.

What we handle

Our core material is used printed circuit boards. Boards contain precious metals such as gold, silver and palladium, and non-ferrous metals such as copper. How much depends on the board type and the mounted components, and it cannot be judged from the outside.

We inspect the actual boards before quoting and explain the basis of the quote. For boards, payment does not wait for laboratory analysis: the price is fixed the moment you accept our quote.

Accepted material

We mainly handle used printed circuit boards. Acceptance conditions depend on type, condition and quantity, so we advise case by case. Start by telling us roughly what you have and how much.

PROCESS

How assessment works

  1. 01InquiryTell us the board type and approximate quantity. We confirm whether we can take it.
  2. 02InspectionWe collect on site or receive the boards by courier, and confirm the quantity on receipt.
  3. 03QuoteWe calculate a quote from the board type, condition and market prices, and present it together with its basis.
  4. 04SettlementThe price is fixed when you accept the quote. Payment does not wait for analysis. If you decline, we return the boards.
