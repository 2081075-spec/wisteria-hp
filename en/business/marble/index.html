FAQ

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the minimum order?

    Slabs can be supplied from a few pieces. Blocks and cut-to-size pieces are quoted according to the quantities of the project.

  • What are the lead times?

    They depend on the producer's stock, whether fabrication is needed, and the sea-freight schedule from Türkiye. We work back from your construction programme and propose when to order.

  • Do you supply slabs only, or cut-to-size as well?

    The producer's factory cuts, polishes and sizes the stone. We supply slabs, blocks, or pieces fabricated to your drawings.

  • Can the stone be bookmatched?

    Yes. The exclusive stones have directional veining suited to bookmatching and vein matching. Tell us the number of pieces and the layout.

  • Is all of the stone Turkish?

    The nine exclusive stones come from the producer's own quarry in İzmir, Türkiye. The 43-stone slab collection also includes stones the producer sources from elsewhere in Türkiye and abroad. We confirm the origin of each stone on request.

  • Can we see samples or the actual slabs?

    We arrange sample pieces and send photographs of the actual slabs before shipment. For large projects, inspection at the factory in Türkiye can be arranged.

  • Who is the producer?

    A natural stone producer in İzmir, Türkiye, with its own quarry and an integrated factory. We share the company name and factory documentation on inquiry.
