ABOUT

Who we are

Wisteria handles resources and products that originate in Japan.

Our work ranges from importing food and feed additives and marble and natural stone directly from overseas manufacturers and recovering metals from used materials, to producing beverages without a heating step and exporting wagyu beef and vehicles. What ties it together is the way we work: we verify what we handle ourselves, then deliver it to the people who need it. We look for things that are undervalued in Japan and trade them while disclosing the basis for our assessment.
