ABOUT

Who we are

Wisteria handles resources and products that originate in Japan.

Our work ranges from recovering metals from used materials and producing beverages without a heating step, to exporting wagyu beef and vehicles and importing food ingredients from overseas manufacturers. What ties it together is the way we work: we verify what we handle ourselves, then deliver it to the people who need it. We look for things that are undervalued in Japan and trade them while disclosing the basis for our assessment.
